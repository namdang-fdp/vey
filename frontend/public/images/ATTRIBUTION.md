# Vey Authentication Visual Artwork

## Asset Details

- **File**: `auth-artwork.webp` (WebP fallback: `auth-artwork.jpg`)
- **Dimensions**: 848 × 1264 (2:3 vertical portrait aspect ratio)
- **Role**: Dominant editorial artwork on the right panel of the Vey authentication split-shell layout (`/login`, `/register`, `/forgot-password`).

## Provenance & Attribution

- **Origin**: Original editorial oil painting created specifically for the Vey project.
- **Inspiration**: Late 19th / early 20th-century Fauvist and Post-Impressionist Mediterranean landscapes (inspired by the coastal pine studies and maritime atmospheres of Louis Valtat at Anthéor and Paul Signac at Saint-Tropez).
- **Composition**: Maritime pine trees overlooking a sunlit Mediterranean turquoise bay, with impasto brushwork, warm golden ochre trail, and cadmium/terracotta cliff accents.
- **License**: Proprietary / Original project asset for Vey. Free of third-party copyright restrictions, external hotlinks, or telemetry.
